Why ResolvedCX

Remote-first, so agents stay and you pay less.

Most outsourcers pack agents into call centers. We've been 100% work-from-home from day one. Without office overhead we can charge clients less and pay agents more, and well-paid agents who don't commute tend to stick around.

Embedded in your stack

Agents train directly with your team and work in your helpdesk (Zendesk, Gorgias and others) and your Slack, so escalations take minutes, not days. No playbook yet? We'll help you write it.

A manager who knows your brand

Every account has an experienced ResolvedCX manager and team leads who handle scheduling, coaching and coverage, so you aren't managing agents yourself.

Industry-leading agent retention

Our agents stay with us far longer than is typical in outsourced support. Better pay, no commute and a real path into Tier 2, team lead and management roles keep them here, so the people who learn your product keep answering your customers.

Quality on your terms

QA is set up around each client: we agree what gets reviewed and how often. Your data stays in your own tools, and we can provide reporting on response time, CSAT and QA whenever you want it.

Flexible by design

Start with a few agents and add more as volume grows or the holidays hit. You're billed only for hours worked, with no long-term commitment.

Fast to staff

With about 350 people already on the team, we fill new seats from people we know. Send us your training materials and we can generally have new agents onboarded within a week.

Lower cost, without cutting corners

No call-center facilities to pay for means lower rates for you and better pay for agents.

FAQ

Questions we hear on every first call.

Where are your agents located?

Most of our agents work from home across the Philippines, with a smaller team in Latin America. We've been fully remote since 2017, with managers and team leads on the ground in the Philippines. Our US team is based in Brooklyn, New York.

How does pricing work?

We bill hourly per agent under a simple statement of work sized to your volume. You're billed only for hours worked, and you can add or reduce agents as your needs change, with no long-term commitment.

How small can we start?

Many clients start with a handful of agents on one channel and grow from there. We size the team to your actual ticket volume, not a minimum headcount.

How quickly can agents start?

Once we have your training materials, we can generally onboard new agents within a week.

Can you help with work beyond customer support?

Yes. We also staff virtual assistants, bookkeeping support, back-office processing and business development roles, recruited and managed the same way as our support teams.

Security

Last updated: September 29, 2026

Privacy PolicyTerms of UseSecurity

Our agents talk to your customers every day, so protecting their information is part of the job. Here's how we handle it.

Your data stays in your systems

Our agents work inside the tools you already use and control: your helpdesk, ecommerce platform and team chat. You create their accounts and set their permissions. We don't copy your customers' information into separate ResolvedCX systems, and you can review or revoke access at any time.

Access is limited and individual

  • Every agent gets a named account. We don't share logins.
  • Agents get only the access their role needs, and we ask clients to set permissions on that basis.
  • We use multi-factor authentication wherever your tools support it.
  • When someone leaves your account or ResolvedCX, we tell you right away and ask that their access be removed immediately.

Our people

  • Everyone on our team agrees to confidentiality obligations before working on a client account.
  • Onboarding includes training on data privacy, handling customer information, and spotting phishing and social engineering.
  • Team leads and managers supervise every account, and our QA reviews check that agents follow your data-handling rules.

Work-from-home standards

Remote work means we set clear standards for every agent's workspace:

  • a private workspace where others can't see the screen;
  • a password-protected, up-to-date computer with security software and an automatic screen lock;
  • a secured home internet connection;
  • no downloading, printing, photographing or storing client or customer data outside your systems.

If something goes wrong

Agents are trained to report suspected security issues to their manager immediately. If we confirm an incident affecting your data, we'll notify you promptly and work with you to contain and fix it, in line with our agreement with you.

Compliance

We comply with the Philippines Data Privacy Act of 2012 and applicable U.S. law. We work within our clients' own compliance programs and can agree to client-specific confidentiality, data-protection and security terms in our services agreements.

Questions or reports

For security questions, or to report a vulnerability or concern, email michael@resolvedcx.com. If you need our security practices for a vendor review, ask your ResolvedCX account manager.
