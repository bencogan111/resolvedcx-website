How we vet agents

Every agent is screened before they reach your queue.

Every agent passes the same screen before they touch a client ticket, then trains on your brand before going live.

  1. Experience check

    Applicants need at least a year of customer service experience and hands-on time in a helpdesk like Zendesk, Gorgias, Freshdesk or Intercom.

  2. Language and typing tests

    We test written and spoken English and typing speed, so replies read naturally and calls sound confident.

  3. Mock tickets

    Candidates answer realistic customer emails using a real brand's policies: a refund request, an angry customer, a confusing charge. We grade them on accuracy, tone and judgment.

  4. Training, then your training

    New hires learn our support standards and common platforms like Zendesk and Shopify. Then we turn your materials into custom lessons and quizzes before they start on your queue.
